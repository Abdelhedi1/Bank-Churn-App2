Data & Upload

Load customer data, prepare features and train the model. Training ends with a fresh scoring run.

1

Load customer data

Required columns: credit_score, country, products_number, credit_card, active_member, estimated_salary. Optional: gender, age, tenure, balance, customer_id, and churn (1 = left) for training.

2

Generate features

Builds risk features (balance vs salary, activity, tenure, age bracket) and an 80/20 train/test split. Features needing a missing column are skipped.

3

Train models

Logistic Regression and LightGBM, 5-fold cross-validation. The best model by AUC is activated. Takes about two minutes.
